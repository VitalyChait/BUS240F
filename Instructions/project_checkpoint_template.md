## Required Summary Elements

1. **Decision Question**
   - Who needs to decide?
   - Which options are being considered?
   - What constraint matters most?

2. **Defined Metric**
   - What does it measure?
   - Units of measurement
   - Included options / timeframes (e.g., specific weeks)
   - Method of aggregation
   - Treatment and explanation of missing observations

3. **Preliminary Claim**
   - One specific, bounded statement supported directly by current evidence.

4. **Static View (Visualization)**
   - Must be readable without opening Tableau.
   - Include:
     - Clear title
     - Units of measure
     - Population / time labels
     - Explicit filters, exclusions, or subset definitions

5. **Unresolved Question**
   - One key uncertainty or diagnostic check that could alter, weaken, or refine your preliminary claim.
